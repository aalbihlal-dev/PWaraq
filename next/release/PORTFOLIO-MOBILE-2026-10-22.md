# Waraq 1.0 beta — Portfolio on phones and tablets, 22 Oct 2026

Test of commit `11ad9ec` (newest HEAD), clean worktree, `pnpm build:site`, served under `/app/` with the website's `_redirects` and CSP (as in the full test). **Test only, no app change.** Devices: iPhone SE (320×568), iPhone 14 (390×664), iPad gen 7 portrait (810×1080) and landscape (1080×810), iPad Pro 11 landscape (1194×834), English and Arabic. **WebKit is not installed on this PC, so Edge (Chromium) ran with each device's viewport, user agent, pixel ratio and touch (`hasTouch`, `isMobile`)**: layout and touch targets are measured faithfully, but Safari-only behaviour is not. Controls are tapped (`tap`), text is typed with the keyboard after a tap (no on-screen keyboard), downloads are opened with MuPDF. Script: `tools/dev/portfolio-mobile.mjs`. Screenshots: `Archive-2026-10-12/screenshots/portfolio-mobile/shots/` (150, one per device × language × step).

## Result
**The whole journey works on every device in both languages (140 of 150 steps pass; the 10 others are one step, "touch drag", marked NOTE: it cannot be tested by emulation).** The layout is the problem: on phones the top row does not fit, and on every device the controls are too small for a finger.

Journey: New portfolio → add 2 PDFs as sections → rename a section (typed) → reorder (Move up / Move down buttons, then a touch drag of the grip) → divider page with a typed note → add a photo → cover (title, names, supervisor typed; logo; design Band; colour) → contents page and page numbers → Export PDF (opened with MuPDF: cover title, contents page, links, page numbers present) → Save .waraq → Close → reopen from Recent.

## Problems, most serious first (each with a proposed fix; nothing was changed)

**1. (Serious, phones) The top row does not fit: seven buttons are off the screen in every Portfolio step, English and Arabic.** On both phones, Undo, Redo, Restore previous version, Close, Theme, Help and more, and Language sit past the screen edge, so a phone user cannot close the portfolio, change language or undo: measured at 390 px the row is 732 px wide, it is clipped (`overflow` is not scrollable) and the page has no sideways scroll, so a finger cannot reach them (only a program scrolling an element into view pans it: that is why some SE screenshots look shifted). At 320 px even "Add a file as a section" is pushed off. The same Undo/Redo/Restore/Close are in view on iPad. Screens: `shots/033-iPhone-14-en-add-two-PDFs-as-sections.png`, `003-iPhone-SE-en-…`. *Proposed fix:* below 520 px keep only logo, title and one primary action in the row and move Undo, Redo, Restore previous version, Theme and Language into the "Help and more" menu (or a second row), keep Close visible as an icon; show "+ Section" (icon and one word) instead of the 4-line "Add a file as a section".

**2. (Serious, all devices) Touch targets are too small: 36 px icon buttons, 40 px colour dots, 36 px text fields; nothing is 44 px.** Phone: Theme, Language, Undo, Redo, Restore, Help (36×36), Close (36 high), the cover colour dots (40×40), the section buttons (Hide pages, Lock, Move up / down, Delete, Drag handle, 36×36), the portfolio name and title fields (36 high). iPad is the same (60 undersized controls on a screen). *Proposed fix:* one rule `@media (pointer: coarse) { button, .btn, input, select, textarea, .dot { min-height: 44px; min-width: 44px } }` with `gap` raised to 8–12 px, so the web layout is not touched.

**3. (Medium, phones) The section's own button strip (Drag handle, title, Move up, Move down, Lock, Hide pages, Replace file, Delete) is wider than the screen**: it scrolls sideways inside a strip (flagged "in a scrolling strip" in the measurements), so Move section down and Delete are off screen until the user finds the scroll. *Fix:* wrap the strip onto two lines on phones, or collapse Lock / Hide / Replace / Delete into a "⋯" menu of the section.

**4. (Medium, all touch devices) Reordering by touch drag could not be confirmed.** The grip uses HTML5 drag and drop (`draggable="true"`, `Portfolio.svelte:285`); synthetic touch, even a long press, does not start it in Chromium, and iOS Safari only starts it after a long press and not reliably in scrolling lists. The Move up / Move down buttons work on every device (rows pass), so reordering is possible. *Fix:* give the grip a pointer-event drag (with `touch-action: none` on the grip) or keep the buttons as the touch way and say so; the page grid's own drag has the same HTML5 mechanism.

**5. (Medium, phones) The options panel is stacked under the pages and takes 40–45 % of the screen** (237 px of 568 at SE, 299 of 664 at iPhone 14), so on a phone the pages and the panel fight for the same small window. It is reachable by scrolling, nothing is cut. On iPad landscape it sits at the side (right in English, left in Arabic) and is fine; iPad portrait stacks it under the pages (486 of 1080 px). *Fix:* on phones turn it into a bottom drawer opened by an "Options" button; in iPad portrait use a side panel of 300 px.

**6. (Low) A label is cut off:** "Choose any colour" (Arabic "اختر أي لون") is clipped at every size; the text does not fit its control. *Fix:* let the label wrap or shorten to "Other colour".

**7. (Low, phones) The page grid is usable**: 5 cards, two per row, 149–160 px wide at 320 px, nothing squeezed; no horizontal page scroll anywhere; no console error or failed request in any of the 150 steps.

## Not covered (limits of this test)
Safari/WebKit itself (not installed: pinch, rubber-band scroll, the iOS file picker and the on-screen keyboard pushing the layout are not tested); a real finger drag (see 4); landscape on iPhone; iPad Pro portrait.
