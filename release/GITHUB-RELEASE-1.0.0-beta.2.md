**Waraq 1.0 beta 2 — PDF studio for Arabic and English. Your files never leave your device.**

Download **`Waraq_1.0.0-beta.2_x64-setup.exe`** below (Windows 10/11, 64-bit, about 29 MB). It installs for your user only, with no administrator rights, and updates itself. It is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show "unknown publisher": choose **More info → Run anyway**. You can also use Waraq in your browser: https://www.pwaraq.com/app/

What it does: organize pages, build portfolios, edit text (Arabic and English), sign, protect, redact, fill forms, OCR scans to searchable text, and comment.

New in beta 2:
- Edit: a **white cover box** (white-out) to write over — for AutoCAD drawings and scans, whose letters are lines, not text. Redact keeps the black box.
- **Ctrl + mouse wheel** over the document zooms the document (View, Edit, Organize, Portfolio), not the whole window.
- Edited drawings keep their file size.
- Edit: new text saved together with a changed line keeps all its letters.

Known limits (beta): about 1 % of edited Arabic lines in unusual PDFs may still need a manual look; OCR of old scanned books is about 63 % word-accurate (clean prints about 97 %); some PDF readers reorder mixed Arabic/English text when copying.

Problems? https://www.pwaraq.com/support or support@pwaraq.com
