# Part 2 — the writers on varied documents, arrangements and scans (owner addition, 21 Oct 2026)

Request: run each writer on different document types, arrangements and scans (not only on blank pages), and check that what Waraq writes lands in the right place on every layout, is visible on scans, and that the OCR text layer still lines up after other edits. **Still audit only: no app source changed.** Everything is regenerated by `tools/arabic/corpus/*` and `tools/arabic/matrix/*`; results are in `tests/audit-arabic/results/matrix-*.json` (one row per check) and `matrix-slices.md`.

## 8. The test corpus (made up, no customer data)

- **13 document types** printed to PDF by Edge from HTML with real Windows fonts (Arial, Tahoma, Traditional Arabic, Sakkal Majalla, Dubai, Segoe UI): letter A4 (1 column), letter US Letter (bilingual labels), invoice (table, bilingual numbers), receipt (80 mm wide, long), contract (numbered clauses, two signature cells), form with boxes (boxes, multiline box, checkbox), landscape table report, newspaper (3 columns), book page A5 (footnotes, tashkeel), slide 16:9 (dark background), poster A3 (gradient), bilingual two-column page, CAD-like drawing (A3 landscape, vector grid, Arabic title block). Sheet: `screenshots\arabic-audit\corpus\base\sheet.png`.
- **11 geometries of each** = 143 PDFs: upright; sheet turned 90 / 180 / 270 (content sideways when seen); **stored turned and shown upright by /Rotate** 90 / 180 / 270 (what a landscape scan or CAD sheet usually is); MediaBox origin (100, 200); CropBox inset 24 pt; offset + crop + turned; stored-turned + offset + crop. The position of every signature blank, form box, phone number and e-mail is known from the HTML (`base/<id>.json`) and mapped through each geometry, so results are judged against the truth, not by eye.
- **26 scans** of those documents with the OCR session's effect library (`tests/fixtures/ocr/scan-fx.mjs`, reused): flatbed 150 and 300 dpi, phone photo (keystone, shade, blur, JPEG), skew 3°, faded, 1-bit, photocopy (noise, pen marks), curved book page. Sheet: `corpus/scans/sheet.png`.
- **11 hybrids**: scans with a **real OCR layer** made the way the app makes it (tesseract.js with the app's `ocr.ts`, `ocr-clean.ts` and `addTextLayer`).
- **5 whole portfolios** built the way `portfolio.ts` builds them: every document type; one letter in every geometry; a paper file in mixed scans; scans with an OCR layer; mixed sheets (turned, offset, cropped, slides, posters, phone photos); plus a phone photo as an image source.
- Audit A's corpus (`tests/fixtures/arabic/`) holds single lines in many fonts and six real public PDFs; it has no document-type, geometry or scan variants, so it was not needed for this part. The OCR session's own scans (`tests/fixtures/ocr/real`, `v3`) are measured for recognition accuracy by that session; here only what Waraq *writes* onto them is judged.

## 11. What this part found (ranked by how often a user would hit it)

### N1. Forms on a page that is shown upright through /Rotate: the Arabic answer is turned and in the wrong corner
- **Hit by:** filling a form from a landscape scan, copier output or CAD-style sheet saved with /Rotate 90 / 180 / 270; also any rotated form. **4 of 4 stored-turned variants fail**, single-line and multiline answers alike; only the page that is simply upright, offset or cropped is right.
- **Repro:** `node tools/arabic/matrix/dbg-forms-view.mjs`; picture `corpus/forms/zoom-notes-rotated.png` (an answer in the notes box: upright page is right; /Rotate 90 the answer is gone from the box, /Rotate 180 it is upside down at the bottom left, /Rotate 270 it is squeezed sideways at the top left). Numbers: `matrix-forms.json`.
- **Root cause:** `forms.ts:56-62` builds the Arabic picture as an appearance with `BBox [0,0,width,height]` from the field's `/Rect` in the page's own space, and never uses the page's `/Rotate` or the field's `/MK /R`; the viewer then turns the picture with the page.
- **Fix (WP1):** give the appearance a `/Matrix` for the rotation (`pageBox().rot` / `/MK /R`) and lay the text out in the field's own upright frame. Local to `forms.ts`.
- Same as issue 3: five answers at once keep only one in **14 of 14** forms in every geometry and every scan.

### N2. Redaction (and any text rewrite) shifts the whole OCR layer on the page
- **Hit by:** anyone who redacts a scanned, OCR-ed page (phone numbers, names): afterwards search hits and selection boxes sit up to several points off their words on the **whole page**, not only near the redaction. **9 of 11 hybrids fail** (only 23–56 % of the other words stay within 3 pt; median shift 2.6–8.9 pt); 3 pages lose neighbouring words as well (receipt: 73.8 % of the neighbouring words kept).
- **Repro:** `node tools/arabic/matrix/dbg-redact-layer.mjs`; picture `corpus/ev/TOP2-ocr-layer-after-redaction.png` (the same page before and after a redaction on another line; boxes = where each word was written).
- **Root cause:** Waraq writes the layer as one `Tz`-scaled `TJ` array per line (`textpage.ts:126-141`, `ocr.ts:41`). MuPDF's redaction rewrites every text object it touches: it re-emits `66.35 Tz` before `Tf` and **recomputes the `TJ` spacing without the scale** (−424.6 becomes −186.9), so each line gets narrower. The picture is untouched (and its pixels are really removed: checked on 4 scans), so the text and the picture no longer agree.
- **Likely also:** replacing a line in the page editor on an OCR-ed page (`editpage.ts:90-100` uses the same `applyRedactions` for the old line; not measured here).
- **Fix (new WP10, `ocr.ts`, `redact.ts`, `editpage.ts`):** either write the layer with one `Tm` per word and no `Tz` (the width then comes from the glyph scale in the matrix, which MuPDF keeps), or remove the layer before redacting and write it again afterwards from the words outside the box.
- Good: numbers, watermark, signature set, highlight, editor text, compress, rebuild (Organize), turn 90° and crop leave the layer exactly where it was (110 of 110 checks; one crop warning on the receipt). Redacting a scan with no layer blacks the picture pixels (10 of 10) and removes the original pixels from the image.

### N3. Sign: labels with extra words are not found, a sideways page is not understood
- **Hit by:** bilingual or two-word labels: «ختم وتوقيع:» (invoice), «Signature / التوقيع:» and «Date / التاريخ:» (letter) are **missed in all 23 PDF variants and 4 hybrids**; plain «التوقيع:» is found (letter A4, form, contract on the upright page). On a **turned sheet** (text sideways when seen, 90 / 270) the blank is put as a horizontal box at the wrong place (the writer assumes horizontal lines). The letter's date line already holds a date and still gets a date spot (12 pages): signing there would write over it. A picture-only scan has no spot at all (the person must place the set by hand): 26 of 26 scans.
- **Repro:** `node tools/arabic/matrix/dbg-spots.mjs invoice-a4--plain letter-letter--plain letter-a4--plain letter-a4--rot90`; `node tools/arabic/matrix/dbg-lines.mjs invoice-a4--plain توقيع` shows MuPDF reads the line as ":ختم وتوقيع" (colon first).
- **Root cause:** `sign.ts:46-48` tests for a colon or blank only 2 characters before and 3 after the word *in the string MuPDF returns*, which for right-to-left lines is in visual order (the colon precedes the word) and, with other words on the line, fails the "little else on the line" test. `blank()` (`sign.ts:68-81`) only looks for typed underscores and the space beside the label; drawn rules (every one of my documents) are not read.
- **Where it works:** once a spot is found, placing the set is right on every geometry and scan: signature, name and date land on the blank clear of existing text in 45 of 48 cases (3 warnings: the set over a ruled table line).
- **Fix (WP4):** look for the colon on both sides (or at either end of the line), count Latin / Arabic label pairs as one label, skip a date label that is followed by a value, read drawn horizontal lines for the blank.

### N4. Page numbers: invisible on dark pages, on the page frame of a drawing, and on shaded phone photos
- **Hit by:** slides and dark covers (grey on dark green: contrast 23 of 255, **14 of 14 slide variants** warn), phone photos with shade (3 of 3), CAD / drawing sheets whose frame runs through the corner (8 warnings: the number touches the frame line, `corpus/ev/TOP2-page-numbers-dark-and-frame.png`), invoices and tables (about 5–20 % of the box over printed content). Position, size and upright orientation are right in all 360 checks on all geometries (rotated, offset, cropped, scanned).
- **Root cause:** `tools.ts:44` writes one fixed colour (grey-green) at 20 pt from the edge, whatever is there.
- **Fix (WP6):** choose black / white by the page's brightness behind the box and move inside a quiet margin (the OCR-style background sampling already exists in `ocr-clean.ts`).

### N5. Comment: sticky-note icon is about 18–25 pt off the clicked point on every page with /Rotate
- **Hit by:** every sticky note on a rotated page: **104 of 104 rotated variants warn** (icon centre 18–26 pt from where it was asked; the icon is anchored by a page corner, not by the click). Rectangles, text boxes and highlights land correctly everywhere (180 / 180, 180 / 180, 113 / 113), including turned, offset, cropped pages and scans.
- **Repro:** `corpus/ev/TOP2-sticky-note-rotated.png` (red square = where asked).
- **Fix (WP2, `annots.ts:79`):** for `note`, shift the rect by the icon size according to the page's rotation (or set the NoRotate flag and anchor the icon's centre).

### N6. OCR layer placement is approximate on varied scans
- On the 9 scans whose geometry equals the document's (flatbed 150 / 300, faded), 70–100 % of the printed words are covered by a layer box (mean overlap 39–74 %); the **receipt** (narrow, monospace, mixed Latin) covers only 37 %. Phone and skewed pages were written with the app's tilt correction and could not be compared with truth. Recognition accuracy itself belongs to the OCR session.

### What passed without a finding
- **Portfolio: 227 of 227**: every source page of the 5 portfolios keeps its seen size and picture (outside the number strip) whatever its geometry or scan kind (upright, turned, stored-turned, offset, cropped, slide, poster, CAD, phone photo, 1-bit, hybrid), gets its number, dividers are sized like their section, contents rows are links, a phone photo as an image source keeps its shape and fits A3.
- **Watermark: 180 / 180**: centred, 45° rising as seen, inside the page on every geometry and scan.
- **Page editor text: 151 right, 29 right but over printed content (the position the test asked for; slides and invoices), 0 wrong** after the box height was computed for wrapped lines (my first run put the box one line high on the 80 mm receipt: test mistake, fixed).
- **Redaction:** found phone numbers (Arabic-Indic and Western digits, spaces) and e-mails on all 47 PDF cases (the 2 misses are OCR digits on hybrids); text removed and boxes black in 46 of 51, neighbours kept on PDFs; the 5 failures are hybrids (N2).
- **Comment:** rectangle, text box and highlight 100 % on every geometry, scan and hybrid.
