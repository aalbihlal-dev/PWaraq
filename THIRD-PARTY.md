# Third-party fonts

| Font | Use | Licence | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tajawal | app interface (Arabic/Latin), generated pages | SIL OFL 1.1 | `public/fonts/Tajawal-*.ttf`, `Tajawal-OFL.txt` |
| Noto Naskh Arabic, Noto Sans Arabic | own-font text editing, OCR test images | SIL OFL 1.1 | `public/fonts/Noto*.ttf`, `Noto-Arabic-OFL.txt` |
| Amiri (Regular, Bold) | Arabic in converted Office documents (desktop app, copied next to LibreOffice at conversion time) | SIL OFL 1.1 | `src-tauri/fonts/Amiri-*.ttf`, `Amiri-OFL.txt` |

The OFL licence texts sit beside the font files. The Office/CAD add-on pack (LibreOffice, LibreDWG) has its own notices in its `THIRD-PARTY-ENGINES.txt`.
